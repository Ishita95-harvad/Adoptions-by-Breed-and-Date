# 🐾 Adoptions by Breed and Date

## 📌 Overview
This dataset contains information on pet adoptions over time, categorized by **breed, adoption date, and relevant factors** influencing adoption rates. It provides valuable insights for **animal shelters, adoption agencies, researchers, and pet lovers**.

## 🔍 Dataset Description
The dataset includes:
- **Adoption Date** – The specific date each pet was adopted.
- **Breed** – Species and breed of adopted pets (e.g., Labrador, Persian Cat).
- **Age** – Age at the time of adoption.
- **Shelter/Source** – Name of the adoption center or foster home.
- **Health Status** – Medical condition at the time of adoption (vaccinated, neutered, etc.).
- **Adopter Demographics** – Optional data on adoption trends by location and household type.
- **Reasons for Adoption** – Factors influencing successful adoptions.

## 📊 Usage & Applications
- **Adoption Trend Analysis** – Understand seasonality and breed popularity.
- **Predictive Modeling** – Forecast future adoption rates.
- **Shelter Resource Optimization** – Improve adoption processes based on data insights.
- **Policy Recommendations** – Support pet welfare initiatives with real adoption statistics.
